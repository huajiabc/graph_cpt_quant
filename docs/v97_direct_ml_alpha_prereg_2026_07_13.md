# v9.7 Direct Cross-Sectional ML Alpha - Pre-Registration

## Scope and permission

This is an offline historical research lane only. It may not change P2, any
paper-live selector, position sizing, canary-live, or real-live permissions.

The question is deliberately direct: can as-of price, volume, funding, OI, and
market-regime features rank the next four-hour relative winners inside the
dynamic monthly Top30 universe?

## Frozen sample and label

- Source: `data/processed/v0_3/perp_pressure_features_all_eligible.parquet`.
- Universe: `universe_dynamic_monthly_top30 == True` only. The static-current
  Top30 field is forbidden because it contains present-universe survivorship.
- Decision timestamps: `minute == 0` and `hour mod 4 == 0`.
- Minimum cross-section: 20 symbols.
- Warmup and finite `future_ret_4h` are required.
- Sample start: 2025-07-01 UTC.
- Complete-month cutoff: 2026-06-01 UTC; the partial June sample is excluded.
- Target: symbol `future_ret_4h` minus the same-timestamp Top30 mean return.
- A four-hour embargo is applied between every training window and evaluation
  month, even though the decision anchors are already non-overlapping.

## Frozen features

Symbol-state block, transformed to same-timestamp percentile ranks using only
the current cross-section:

- `ret_15m`, `ret_1h`, `ret_4h`;
- `volatility_1h`, `volatility_4h`;
- `volume_z_1h`, `volume_z_4h`;
- `funding_z`, `funding_percentile`;
- `oi_value_delta_z_1h`, `oi_value_delta_z_4h`;
- `oi_value_delta_1h_percentile`, `oi_value_delta_4h_percentile`;
- `ret_4h_percentile`, `volume_1h_percentile`,
  `volume_4h_percentile`;
- log turnover rank.

Market block, carried as common as-of context:

- `btc_ret_1h`, `btc_ret_4h`, `btc_volatility_4h`,
  `btc_volatility_percentile`.

Symbol identity, current/future labels, event outcomes, state clusters, and all
post-entry fields are forbidden as inputs.

## Frozen model ladder

1. `momentum_rank`: `ret_4h_percentile` only; no fit.
2. `ridge`: median imputation, standardization, Ridge alpha 10.
3. `xgb_shallow`: 240 trees, depth 3, learning rate 0.03, subsample 0.75,
   column sample 0.75, minimum child weight 50, alpha 1, lambda 10, histogram
   tree method, seed 20260713.
4. `xgb_shuffled`: the identical XGBoost specification trained on labels
   permuted separately inside each training month.
5. Three fixed XGBoost ablations: no momentum/volume block, no funding/OI
   block, and no BTC-regime block.

No hyperparameter tuning is allowed after reading validation or holdout output.
Neural networks, stacking, Optuna, and symbol embeddings are outside this
round. They require evidence that the shallow tree already beats Ridge.

## Walk-forward split

Expanding prior-month-only training with a six-month minimum:

- 2026-01 and 2026-02: development walk-forward;
- 2026-03 and 2026-04: validation;
- 2026-05: final historical holdout;
- 2026-06: excluded because the stored month is incomplete.

Every prediction is generated by a model that has not seen its evaluation
month or the four hours immediately preceding it.

## Portfolio conversion

- Equal-weight Top5 by predicted score every four hours.
- Positions may carry across adjacent anchors when still selected.
- Turnover is the fraction of names replaced since the prior anchor.
- Cost stress applies 10, 20, 30, and 50 bps to a fully replaced portfolio.
- Focal metric: cumulative Top5 return minus contemporaneous Top30 mean minus
  turnover-adjusted 20bp cost.
- Report IC, hit rate, turnover, drawdown proxy, monthly contribution, symbol
  contribution, and cost stress.

## Controls

- 200 random Top5 paths with the identical timestamp coverage and cost model.
- `xgb_shuffled` monthly label-permutation control.
- Ridge and momentum complexity baselines.
- Fixed feature-block ablations.

## Decision gates

`xgb_shallow` is only a complexity candidate when all are true:

1. validation and holdout net20 excess are positive;
2. validation and holdout net20 excess both exceed Ridge;
3. validation and holdout mean IC both exceed Ridge;
4. full-OOS net30 excess is positive;
5. at least three of five complete OOS months have positive net20 excess;
6. no single month contributes more than 35% of positive full-OOS net20;
7. the XGBoost result exceeds the 90th percentile of random Top5 paths;
8. XGBoost beats the shuffled-label control;
9. removing any one feature block does not reveal that the full result depends
   entirely on a single unstable block.

Even if every gate passes, this round remains `research_candidate_only`
because the final holdout contains one complete month. Failure to beat Ridge
means there is no present case for added model complexity. Failure of Ridge,
XGBoost, and momentum after costs means the direct bar-level alpha lane is
rejected under this specification.
